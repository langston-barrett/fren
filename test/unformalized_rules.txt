# The rules of the reference's **Legality rules** and **Dynamic semantics**
# that no requirement formalizes yet (`Reqs/dev/Style#rules-and-requirements`),
# one per line, in a section for each chapter, in the chapter's order. The
# Measure step of a chapter group (#620) lists its chapters' rules, in
# sections marked " (Interlock pending)"; its Interlock step gives each a
# requirement, removes its line and the mark. A rule that is to keep no
# requirement says why, after ` -- `, which every line of a section without
# the mark must. A rule whose markers link only part of what it states is
# listed too, with ` -- partly formalized` (or `: ` and what is missing
# after it), until they link all of it. The
# `ruleReqs` job fails on a rule of those labels that has no requirement and
# is not listed, and on a listed one that has one (but for `partly
# formalized`), is of another label, is in another chapter's section, or is
# no rule.
