Evaluation does not confirm a counterexample under recursive functions' contracts.

Recursive applications are opaque, with one step of their defining equations.
A solver model may therefore give them values that the real definitions do not
produce. Fren evaluates the counterexample's inputs before reporting it.
If evaluation does not confirm the failure, because it refutes it or cannot
evaluate it, as when a part is undecided (`Reqs/Eval#confirming-models`),
the check is Inconclusive. Strengthen the named recursive functions'
contracts to make the needed facts available
(`Reqs/Lang/Recursion#counterexamples-and-completeness`).
